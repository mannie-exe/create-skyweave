# Create: Skyweave 0.7.0

Compose ordinary villages from CTOV, Better Villages and Towns and Towers; add
selected YUNG structures and Geophilic ecology. Minecraft remains 1.21.1 and
NeoForge remains 21.1.265.

- Add YUNG's Better Desert Temples, Better Jungle Temples, Better Mineshafts,
  Better Witch Huts and Better Ocean Monuments with their required API.
  Strongholds and Nether fortresses retain their existing forms. Witch huts and
  circles share one island encounter budget as eligible alternatives.
- Use Lithostitched and CTOV's regional/size organization for one ordinary-village
  schedule, with selected Better Villages/Towns and Towers pieces and coherent
  regional layouts. Bound donor roles without concatenating their loot tables.
  Sky Villages remain a separate floating settlement system.
- Exclude ocean ships, obsolete CTOV/Wares loot resources and malformed optional
  computer-lab injections. Repair four missing Towns and Towers villager pools
  using existing regional adults, without adding professions or trades.
- Add Geophilic decoration over existing biomes and climate. Preserve native
  surface/heightmap placement rather than adding a second elevation remap.
- Update Compatibility Patches to 0.27.0 for supported native assembly, YUNG
  placement/support, coherent lake roofs and rivers that end before detached
  fragments. Normal-sized Lagoon treatments include the broad coastal cove.
- Collect owned Compat configuration and shader recipes under `config/skyweave/`.
  Keep foreign mod settings at their native paths and preserve personal edits
  during the explicit stopped-instance move.
- Set new client defaults to view12/simulation10 and the server template to
  view16/simulation12. Existing seeded player settings remain installation-local.
- Keep Atlas captures isolated and source-owned, including the nested config
  layout and complete authored journal set.

**Create a fresh world for these terrain changes.** Existing generation is not
converted. Native checks cover complete/reopened composed villages and selected
structures, supported doors/beds, water stages and ticks, optional loading and
configuration. These finite samples do not establish villager pathfinding,
every layout or survival balance. Monuments retain their habitat requirements;
an eligible island monument was not demonstrated in the bounded sample.
