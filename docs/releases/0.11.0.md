# Create: Skyweave 0.11.0

Add capability-based FTB quests, repair bounded discovery and improve map and
inventory controls. Minecraft remains 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.265; Skyweave:
Compatibility Patches is updated to 0.30.0.

- Populate FTB Quests with 74 non-linear quests in nine capability/reference
  chapters, covering the previous journal subjects and client conveniences.
  Use modest manually claimed supplies, explicit personal/team reward scope,
  native action evidence and supported item alternatives. Keep the legacy
  Questlog book/progress intact during the trial.
- Replace Questlog authoring with FTB chapter/task/reward/layout/localization
  authoring in Atlas. Keep stable native IDs, generated-file conflict detection,
  coordinated saves and real native parser/export verification.
- Make acquisition/default routes aware of dimension access, preset generation,
  features, structures and source provenance. Preserve Overworld salvage and
  useful processing alternatives; keep blocked and unclassified routes visible.
  This does not claim travel/resource budgets or a survival-complete economy.
- Search managed structures and Sky Villages through their actual candidate
  contracts, preserve saved starts and make incomplete coverage explicit.
  Guide marked-world native structure lookup to the bounded command; biome and
  POI lookup remain unchanged. Structure rarity and terrain identity are retained.
- Close native Nether activation/entry in marked worlds, including integrated
  servers, while preserving ordinary fire and stored dimension/player data.
  Recover saved Nether logins/respawns through allowed native destinations.
- Choose a safe nearby companion island for a new world's shared initial spawn.
  Preserve mature-world spawn, native personal beds and existing player positions;
  custom team starts/home/reset policies remain deferred.
- Extend Hoofprint zoom and repair FTB's creative-inventory return with IPN.
  Hide IPN settings/overlay-editor buttons while retaining the three sort modes
  and useful inventory features. No magenta-indicator artwork change is included.
- Consolidate maintained documentation, scripts and resource generation checks.

No world reset is required. Keep existing worlds, player/team progress, BlueMap
and DH data. The prison dimension, team-start allocation and general performance
work remain deferred. Native, browser and release-archive qualification are
recorded in the development verification guides with their coverage limits.
