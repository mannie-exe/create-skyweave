# Create: Skyweave 0.13.0

Update Skyweave: Compatibility Patches to 0.32.0 for supported village layouts, island-aware structure foundations, discovery repairs and Wayfinder tuning. Minecraft remains 1.21.1 and NeoForge remains 21.1.265.

- Fit villages against their actual foundation footprint and preserve connected inhabited buildings. Try more bounded placement alternatives before accepting trimmed settlements, while retaining existing family rates and donor variety.
- Keep sandy island foundation blends sandy. The native support fill remains active; existing terrain and structures are not repainted or relocated.
- Deduplicate source/reference aliases in structure searches and map presentation, preserve distinct neighboring layouts, and retain stable coordinates after native jigsaw reload. Qualify incomplete search coverage explicitly.
- Add configurable village support, retention and work limits to the existing World Generation settings. Defaults require 90% support within three blocks, retain at least 65% of inhabited buildings and try up to eight ranked sites.
- Default Wayfinder feedback and warp cooldown off, with configurable upward-only safe-placement lookup of 16 blocks. Existing explicit settings remain authoritative.
- Require operator permission on the islands and structures command branches, independently of other commands under the shared root.

Generation behavior and its saved identity change. Use a fresh world for the new generation; existing worlds are not silently converted. A world reset is an operator action, not part of installing the update. Discovery repairs do not require deleting map records or personal waypoints.

Matched bounded samples preserve village allowances and commit eight villages versus five on the baseline across eight positive clusters from two seeds. This is not a world-wide encounter-rate estimate. Native checks and their limits are recorded in Compat's verification guide. No content mods are added or removed.
